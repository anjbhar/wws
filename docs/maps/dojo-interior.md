# Neighborhood dojo interior

Map002 is a 17 × 15 map with 48 × 48 pixel tiles and an approximately 15 × 11 floor envelope inside its walls. The center remains open for practice. Warm red-brown floorboards, cream plaster and shoji match the courtyard. A small rear alcove holds a scroll and offering shelf, with a bokken rack at left and a low chest and indigo cushions at right. The centered recessed entrance has a wooden step.

Artwork: built-in imagegen, using the courtyard as a style reference. Original generation, exact prompts, original 816 × 720 artwork and export metadata are retained in `docs/art/dojo-interior-v1/`. The current `DojoInterior_B-v2` sheet adds a localized entrance correction, retained with its exact prompt and exterior door reference in `docs/art/dojo-interior-v2/`. It replaces the gravel opening with a smooth recessed landing, wooden step, open vertical-board door leaves and a red-brown sill matching the exterior double door and veranda. Pixels outside the entrance rectangle (312,576)–(504,720) are unchanged. The sheet packs the artwork into ordinary MZ B tiles; this is a baked map illustration rather than a reusable modular furniture tileset. Each cell has its own passage flag. Walls and equipment block movement; floor and entrance are walkable.

Walk north into the courtyard dojo door from the center veranda tile (12,4). Player-touch event 5 on the door (12,3) enters Map002 at (8,13), facing north. The recessed landing is three cells wide; the narrower bottom doorway has a center player-touch exit at (8,14), returning to courtyard (12,4), facing south. Its flanking jamb cells are blocked. Other courtyard events are preserved. Existing data before this addition is backed up under `backups/before-dojo-interior-v1/`; the entrance correction has its own `backups/before-dojo-entrance-v2/` snapshot.

Re-export the original map with the bundled Python runtime and Pillow: `tools/export-dojo-interior.py <original-source.png>`. Then apply the current entrance with `tools/update-dojo-entrance.py docs/art/dojo-interior-v2/entrance-source.png`. No plugins or engine changes are required. Artwork dimensions, tile references, collision reachability and transfer events are checked without launching the game. Character contact with furnishings and the entrance transition still need manual playtesting.
